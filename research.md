# Current Research Interests

My research focuses on developing machine learning methods to design and understand proteins. Specifically, my work aims to engineer novel and complex functions by combining advances in deep learning and experimental methods. Some of my current interests include: 

- Generative models and pretraining for proteins and chemistry
- Machine learning for protein engineering
- Experimental methods that increase the scale or fidelity of measurements
